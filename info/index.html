The Workflow

01. Import Data

Simply drag and drop your GeoTIFF files. Our engine processes high-resolution elevation data directly in your browser with no server latency.

02. Visualize

Experience your data in first-person. Apply vertical exaggeration, dynamic lighting, and custom color ramps to reveal hidden patterns.

03. Export

Generate 4K production-ready visuals. Export cinematic video sequences, high-resolution snapshots, or industry-standard 3D GLB models.

Precision Smoothing

Our proprietary smoothing algorithm allows you to choose between raw, sharp topographical data and professional, interpolated surfaces. This is critical for distinguishing between natural geological formations and man-made structures.
